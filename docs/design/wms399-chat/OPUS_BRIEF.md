Requirements must cover at least:

- Main conversation and focused thread, reply/edit/delete permissions and local demo send, reactions, mention rendering, anchors/deep links, unread separator and honest sent/sending/failed/read states.
- Internal note vs seller-visible reply, explicit recipients/visibility; show no-leak seller view.

Actions route to demonstrable document preview or clearly marked existing-flow handoff, no silent stock mutation.
- Cross-chat search with filters for seller, date, text, files/document numbers; results navigate to message/thread and preserve context.
- Notifications center, mentions, mute/digest preferences and permission-denied push state; explicitly proposal with no system notifications sent.
- Conversation details/participants/access, task-like follow-up if you find useful without inventing a second stock workflow; explain who can invite/change audience.
